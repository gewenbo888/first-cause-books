# First Cause · 第一推动 — A Bilingual Atlas of the Landmark Chinese Popular-Science Series

A bilingual scholarly companion to **「第一推动」丛书** (the *First Cause* series), the landmark popular-science series launched in 1992 by 湖南科学技术出版社 (Hunan Science & Technology Press) — the publishing program that, over thirty years, carried Schrödinger, Crick, Penrose, Thorne, Chandrasekhar, Davis, Nesse & Williams, Brockman, Gribbin, Kippenhahn, Coveney & Highfield, and Laughlin into ordinary Chinese bookshops, in serious translations, with confident covers, at prices an undergraduate could afford.

This site is not the full series — it is one specific collection of **thirteen books** drawn from it (the contents of the [cs-zone/first-cause-books](https://github.com/cs-zone/first-cause-books) repository), organized into five subjects and read as an atlas of what the series, at its best, has tried to do.

## What's on the shelf

**I · Cosmos** — Kippenhahn *100 Billion Suns* · Gribbin *Universe: A Biography* · Thorne *Black Holes and Time Warps*
**II · Physics** — Laughlin *A Different Universe* · Coveney & Highfield *The Arrow of Time*
**III · Life** — Crick *The Astonishing Hypothesis* · Schrödinger *What Is Life?* · Davies *Cracking the Genome*
**IV · Synthesis** — Brockman *The Next Fifty Years* · Penrose *The Emperor's New Mind* · Chandrasekhar *Shakespeare, Newton, and Beethoven* · Davis *The Universal Computer*
**V · Medicine** — Nesse & Williams *Why We Get Sick*

Each book gets a full bilingual entry: Chinese and English titles, author, original year and publisher, a ~200-word summary, a "why it matters" callout, and a one-line key idea in the sub-series accent color.

## Visualizations

- **CosmicShelf** — atmospheric hero canvas: a starfield with the 13 books as glowing book-stars on a faint constellation lattice
- **SeriesArc** — all 13 books plotted along their original publication years, 1944 → 2007, with one row per sub-series
- **ThemeWeb** — 9 cross-cutting themes (cosmos, time, emergence, life, mind, computation, creativity, medicine, futures) connected to the books that speak to them
- **ReadingRoadmap** — six curated reader paths through the 13 books, each in 2–3 steps
- **AuthorsConstellation** — 15 authors as a constellation, with birth-year axis, disciplines, and Nobel badges where they apply
- **ConceptCompass** — 8-axis octagonal radar of the deep concepts the series circles (entropy, code, gravity, mind, universality, life, aesthetics, emergence) × 3 reader profiles
- **LibraryRecursion** — 8 reading depths from "open the cover for 1 minute" to "find the next person you will hand one of these books to"

## Stack

Next.js 14 (App Router) · React 18 · TypeScript · Tailwind CSS. Zero runtime visualization dependencies — every diagram is hand-built with 2D canvas and inline SVG. Bilingual via a lightweight React language context. Display typography in **Cormorant Garamond**; Chinese in **Noto Serif SC**.

## About

An interpretive companion to a collection — not the books themselves. **Buy the originals; the 「第一推动」 series is one of the great cultural projects of contemporary Chinese popular-science publishing.** Part of the [Psyverse](https://psyverse.fun) portfolio by [Gewenbo](https://psyverse.fun).
